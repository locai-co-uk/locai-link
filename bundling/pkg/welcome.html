Welcome to the Loc.ai Link Installer

Loc.ai Link is the on-device Agent runtime that turns this Mac into an inference node on your local network.

Once installed, this device becomes an OpenAI-compatible endpoint your organisation owns — keeping inference on-device and your data in custody.

  • Runs entirely on your hardware — no cloud round-trip.
  • Joins your Control Plane as a managed node.
  • Removable at any time from System Settings.
